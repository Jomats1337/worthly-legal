Privacy Policy

Effective 3 November 2026

This policy explains how Jomats FZCO handles information when you use Worthly, including its financial lessons, practice portfolio, Ask AI and these legal pages.

Jomats FZCO
Building A2, Dubai Silicon Oasis, DDP; Dubai, Dubai; United Arab Emirates.
Privacy and support: support@worthly.me

Worthly uses a guest identity to save your progress. Ask AI is optional and sends information to OpenAI only after the app asks for your permission. Practice balances and trades are virtual. You can delete your account and data in the app.

1. Information we process

  • Guest account and profile. A generated account identifier and session credentials connect your app to its server records. We process your chosen display name, avatar, language and profile preferences. Email/password registration is disabled in this release. A profile photo you select stays on your device; choosing an image as an AI attachment is a separate action.
  • Onboarding and investing preferences. Your age range, investing experience, learning and financial goals, concerns, barriers and interests are saved with your guest account. The optional investing-style assessment includes your time horizon, ability to handle an unexpected expense, savings goals, experience and risk preferences; we save the answers and result. These are learning preferences, not verification of your finances.
  • Learning and practice activity. We process lesson and quiz progress, saved pages, reading positions, learning time, rewards, activity dates, simulated orders, holdings and virtual balances. We also keep usage counts to apply feature limits. An optional device-state backup may include additional saved app preferences, watchlist items, feedback and conversation state.
  • AI messages and files. We store the messages you send, generated replies, chat history and images or PDF documents you choose to upload. Upload records include the file name, type, size and time; PDFs may be converted to text for processing. We do not record your voice to provide lesson narration.
  • Subscriptions and usage. Apple and Adapty provide product, transaction, subscription and entitlement information. We use it to validate paid access and restore eligible purchases. We do not receive your payment card or bank account details.
  • Technical information. Our server logs request IP addresses, paths, timestamps and status codes for security and troubleshooting. Adapty processes the app installation ID, identifier for vendor, profile/user IDs, device model, OS and app versions, locale, time zone, storefront and subscription/paywall activity. This release does not register devices for remote push notifications; local reminders are controlled on your device.
  • Support. If you contact us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send, such as a problem description or screenshot.

2. Why we use information

We use information to provide and synchronize your learning experience; show your investing-style result and relevant app preferences; operate the virtual portfolio and AI features; verify subscriptions; apply limits; maintain security and reliability; understand purchase and paywall performance; answer support requests; and meet applicable legal obligations. We do not sell personal information or use it for cross-app advertising tracking.

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, we rely on providing the service you request, our legitimate interests in security and service operation, applicable legal obligations, and your consent where required. Optional sharing with OpenAI uses a separate permission choice in the app.

3. Ask AI and OpenAI

OpenAI is the AI provider used by this release. After you allow sharing and submit an AI request, Worthly sends your message and relevant conversation history through our server to OpenAI. This can include the images you attach, text extracted from PDFs, the lesson you ask about, selected asset information and, for portfolio questions, your simulated holdings and virtual balance. Automated safety checks also send relevant input and generated output to OpenAI for moderation. We use these checks and educational instructions to reduce harmful responses; they do not guarantee that every response is safe, accurate or suitable.

The app asks before your first AI submission. You may decline and continue using lessons and the practice portfolio. Review or withdraw permission in Settings → AI data sharing → Revoke permission. Withdrawal stops new AI submissions and cancels pending work where possible; it cannot recall information already received by OpenAI. It does not delete existing chat history. Use chat deletion or account deletion to remove data held by Worthly.

OpenAI states that API data is not used for model training by default. Its standard abuse-monitoring logs may retain prompts, responses and related information for up to 30 days, with exceptions for legal or safety needs. Different processing, caching and image-safety rules can apply; we do not promise zero retention by OpenAI. Read OpenAI's API data controls for its current rules. Do not submit passwords, payment credentials, government ID documents or personal information that is unnecessary for your learning question.

4. Other providers and international processing

  • Apple processes App Store payments and subscriptions under its own terms and privacy practices.
  • Adapty manages paywalls, purchase activity and subscription access. Our integration disables advertising-identifier collection, explicit IP-address collection and attribution; installation/profile identifiers and technical information described above still apply. See Adapty's end-user privacy policy.
  • Twelve Data supplies market information requested by asset symbol. Worthly requests it from our server; it does not send your guest profile, chat messages or entire portfolio to Twelve Data.
  • Hosting and infrastructure providers process data needed to run Worthly and store its service records.
  • GitHub Pages hosts these legal pages. GitHub receives web-request information and logs visitors' IP addresses for security even when visitors are not signed in. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not add advertising or analytics scripts to these pages. Loading the Worthly logo also requests an image from our server.

Our company is based in the United Arab Emirates. Service providers may process information in other countries, including the United States, where privacy rules may differ from those where you live. Contact us for questions about the providers handling your data and applicable transfer protections.

5. Storage, deletion and backups

Your guest account and its active service records are retained until you delete them or request removal. Inactive guest accounts do not currently expire automatically. You can delete a chat conversation; uploaded files are stored separately and remain until separately removed or your account is deleted. Turning off AI permission does not remove these records.

Use Profile → Worthly server → Your data → Delete Account & Data to delete the current guest account, its server records and uploaded files, and reset the current profile on your device. We queue deletion of its Adapty profile and retry if that service is unavailable. A minimal account identifier and deletion status remain while that request is pending. Deleting a guest account does not cancel an Apple subscription or erase records that Apple or OpenAI retain under their own rules.

Routine database and file backups are scheduled daily and rotate after approximately 15 days, so deleted data can remain in those backups during that period. Manually created recovery copies, including copies kept outside the server, are not covered by that automatic rotation and may remain until separately removed. They are restricted recovery copies, not the active account. Contact us about deletion requests involving recovery copies.

Web-server logs use daily rotation with up to 14 archived files; this is not a guaranteed deletion date for every log entry. Application logs use size-based rotation. Support correspondence and records needed for a dispute, security investigation or legal obligation may be retained separately; contact us for help with a specific record.

6. Your controls and requests

You can change profile preferences, manage local notification permissions in iOS, decline or withdraw AI sharing, delete chat history and delete your account. Depending on your location, you may also have rights to access, correct, export or delete personal information, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, or complain to the relevant privacy authority. Contact support@worthly.me or write to the address above. We may need information to confirm the request relates to your guest account; never send your session credentials or passwords.

We use access controls and HTTPS to protect service data. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. Protect your device and avoid including unnecessary personal information in messages or attachments.

7. Age eligibility

Worthly is for people 13 and older. Users under 18 need permission from a parent or legal guardian. Ask AI additionally requires you to meet any higher minimum age for digital consent that applies where you live; parental permission does not override this AI age requirement. The app asks you to confirm eligibility and permission before AI sharing. Worthly is not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child below the applicable minimum age has provided personal information, contact us so we can investigate and address it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Worthly's features or data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version. Where a change requires renewed consent, we will ask for it before the affected optional processing.
